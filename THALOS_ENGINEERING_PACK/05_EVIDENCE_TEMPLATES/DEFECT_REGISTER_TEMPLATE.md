Severity guidance:
- Critical: likely compromise, unauthorized material action, irreversible data loss, or core system unavailable.
- High: major feature broken, significant data integrity/security risk, or unsafe execution.
- Medium: important workflow degraded with workaround.
- Low: cosmetic or minor usability issue.

Never close a defect without a reproducible regression test or a documented reason why automated reproduction is not feasible.
